Nonprofit Eligibility & Participation Standards

LINK Community Hub™ is a community connection and resource ecosystem. It is not a fundraising or donation-processing platform.

  • Participation is free for eligible organizations.
  • The organization must be recognized as a 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization.
  • The organization must serve residents or communities within the Lake Norman region or an approved surrounding service area.
  • Political campaigns, candidates, candidate committees, political parties, PACs and partisan campaign entities are not eligible.
  • Organizations may share community needs, events, resources needed, volunteer opportunities, programs, expertise needed, collaboration opportunities and ways businesses or residents can engage.
  • LINK does not collect or distribute charitable donations on behalf of participating organizations.
  • All organizations and submitted information are subject to LINK review and verification before publication.
  • Participation does not constitute endorsement by LINK Community Hub™.
