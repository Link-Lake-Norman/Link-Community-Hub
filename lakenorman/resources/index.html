BUSINESS RESOURCE EXCHANGE

Available local resources.

Approved local nonprofits can request available resources posted by participating businesses. LINK makes the introduction; the business and nonprofit handle the transfer directly.

How it works

LINK Community Hub™ connects businesses with approved local nonprofits. LINK does not collect funds, hold donations, store inventory, arrange delivery, determine tax deductibility, issue donation receipts or take possession of donated property. Resource availability is controlled by the participating business.
