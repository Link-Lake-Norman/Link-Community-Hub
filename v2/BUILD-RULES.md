## Data Rules

DO NOT invent nonprofit information.

DO NOT guess links.

Every opportunity must ultimately reference a verified nonprofit ID.

Nonprofit participation is FREE.

Nonprofits register and manage information through LINK.

Individuals are private.

Students are private.

Volunteer registrations are private.

Student eligibility for an opportunity must be explicitly approved.
