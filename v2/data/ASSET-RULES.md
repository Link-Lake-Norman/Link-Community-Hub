# LINK Community Hub™ V2 Asset Rules

## APPROVED HOMEPAGE

The approved design uses:

- ONE wide cinematic hero image
- dark navy left overlay
- community / people grouped naturally on the right
- TOGETHER, WE CAN DO on the left
- Extraordinary Things in gold script
- thin impact band directly below hero
- six compact engagement cards
- compact Real Needs section
- real nonprofit logo rail
- compact Living Ecosystem section
- gold closing CTA

DO NOT:
- create hero collages
- create floating image boxes
- reinterpret the approved layout
- use random images in major visual positions
- use text names as the final nonprofit logo treatment
- expose student or volunteer identities
- migrate old demo videos
- migrate Seasons of Giving LKN
